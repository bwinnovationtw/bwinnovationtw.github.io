Add and switch to 5RowKeys

  1. Open Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards.
  2. Tap Add New Keyboard and select 5RowKeys.
  3. Open a text field, touch and hold the globe key, and choose 5RowKeys.

Leave Allow Full Access off. 5RowKeys processes typing on your device. Plus keyboard features also work without Full Access.

iOS switches to its system keyboard in password fields and may do so in other fields that do not allow third-party keyboards. To switch back, open a regular text field and choose 5RowKeys from the globe menu.

Choose and switch languages

Open 5RowKeys → Languages and select the language you want to type in. Free supports one language at a time, with English selected initially. Plus lets you enable more languages and use Edit to reorder them.

In the keyboard, tap the language code above the number row to choose an enabled language. Tap the globe to move through your enabled languages in order, then to the next iOS keyboard. Touch and hold the globe to open the iOS keyboard list.

If iOS shows Multiple languages next to 5RowKeys even when you have selected just one, check the language code on the keyboard. The system label describes the extension, not your current selection.

Restore or manage Plus

Restore a purchase

Open the 5RowKeys app, select Discover Plus or 5RowKeys Plus, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you used to buy Plus. If features are missing after a renewal, reopen the app first to refresh access.

Manage or cancel a subscription

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then choose Subscriptions → 5RowKeys. Subscriptions renew automatically until canceled. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Lifetime is a one-time purchase.
