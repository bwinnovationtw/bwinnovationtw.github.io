Try, activate, and add the keyboard

  1. Try it in the app. After downloading 5RowKeys, use the onboarding preview or open Try 5RowKeys before installing from the app's home screen. Tap the language code above the keys to try any of the 15 options. No purchase or keyboard setup is required.
  2. Activate or restore Plus. Open Discover Plus or 5RowKeys Plus in the app. Start a subscription or an eligible trial, buy Lifetime, or tap Restore Purchases if you've already purchased Plus.
  3. Add the keyboard in iOS. Go to Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard → 5RowKeys. You can leave Allow Full Access off.
  4. Switch with the globe. In another app that supports custom keyboards, open a regular text field. Touch and hold the globe key and select 5RowKeys.

Your typing stays on your device. Full Access is not required.

Choose and switch languages

In the free preview, tap the language code above the number row to try any keyboard option. Trying a language doesn't change the languages you've enabled for the installed keyboard.

With Plus, open 5RowKeys → Languages to choose which languages are available in other apps. Use Edit to reorder them. Tap the language code on the installed keyboard to pick one, or tap the globe to cycle through your order and then switch to the next iOS keyboard.

If iOS shows Multiple languages next to 5RowKeys even when you have selected just one, check the language code on the keyboard. The system label describes the extension, not your current selection.

Restore or manage Plus

Restore a purchase

Open the 5RowKeys app, select Discover Plus or 5RowKeys Plus, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you used to buy Plus. If the keyboard stops working in other apps after a renewal, reopen 5RowKeys first to refresh access.

Manage or cancel a subscription

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then choose Subscriptions → 5RowKeys. Subscriptions renew automatically until canceled. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Lifetime is a one-time purchase.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need Plus to try 5RowKeys?

No. The in-app preview is free for all 15 options. Typing in other apps that support custom keyboards requires an active Plus subscription, an active trial, or a lifetime purchase. If your access expires, the free preview remains available.

Do I need to turn on Full Access?

No. Typing, suggestions, swipe input, and emoji search are processed on your device without Full Access. 5RowKeys doesn't record or upload a typing history.

Why does the system keyboard appear instead?

iOS uses its own keyboard for password fields and other fields or apps that restrict custom keyboards. In a regular text field in a supported app, touch and hold the globe and select 5RowKeys again.
