5RowKeys

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy covers the 5RowKeys app and its 5RowKeys keyboard extension.

Your typing stays on your device

The keyboard processes the text available around your cursor on your device to provide typing, spelling, word completions, next-word suggestions, language conversion, and swipe typing. Next-word suggestions use bundled language data and do not learn from or build a history of your typing. We do not record or transmit your typed text, swipe paths, or emoji search queries. This information is used only as needed for the current typing interaction.

The keyboard does not contact our servers, and we do not operate a typing or prediction server. Full Access is not required. iOS may use its system keyboard in password fields or other fields that do not allow third-party keyboards.

Settings and emoji preferences

The app and keyboard store preferences locally, including your selected languages and their order, typing options, custom symbol layouts, split-layout preference, recently used emoji, and preferred skin tones. These preferences are used to provide the keyboard features you choose.

The app shares settings and purchase-access information with its keyboard through an on-device App Group container. Typed text is not stored in that container. Local preferences may be included in device backups according to your Apple backup settings.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes in-app purchases, subscriptions, free-trial eligibility, and payments. The app uses Apple's StoreKit service to verify access and may refresh purchase status while it is open or when iOS permits a background update.

The app saves verified access information locally, such as the product you purchased and its expiry date, so the keyboard can enable paid features without Full Access. We do not receive or store payment-card details, and we do not require a separate 5RowKeys account or operate a purchase-account server.

Apple handles purchase records under Apple's Privacy Policy. You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple Account settings. Removing the app does not cancel an active subscription.

Advertising, tracking, and analytics

We do not include advertising, cross-app tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. Apple may provide developers with diagnostics, TestFlight feedback, or App Store reports under Apple's terms and your Apple privacy settings. We do not use these services to record your typing.

Retention and your choices

Typed text, swipe paths, and search queries are not retained as a typing history. Language and typing preferences, recent emoji, and purchase-access information remain on your device until you change them or remove the app and its extension. Apple retains purchase records under its own policies.

You can change your enabled languages and typing preferences in the app. You can remove the keyboard in Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards and remove locally stored app data by deleting the app. Backup copies are managed through your device's backup settings.

Updates to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated with a new date. The current policy is available inside the app and at the 5RowKeys privacy-policy page.

Contact and terms

For support or privacy questions, use the developer support information on the app's App Store listing or the developer website.

Use of the app and its in-app purchases is governed by Apple's Standard End User License Agreement.
