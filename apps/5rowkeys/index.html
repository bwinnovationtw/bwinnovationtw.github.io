5RowKeys

Two displays. Five rows.

The numbers stay directly above the letters. On iPhone Duo, the keyboard adapts to the compact outer display and wider inner display, in portrait or landscape.

On-device suggestions

Complete a word or pick the next one from the suggestion strip. Next-word suggestions work in all 12 supported Latin-alphabet languages.

Accents and previews

See a character preview as you press a key, or touch and hold a letter to choose an alternate character.

Easy keyboard switching

Choose your languages in the app. Use the globe to cycle through enabled languages and switch to your other iOS keyboards.
