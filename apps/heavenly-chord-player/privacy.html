Heavenly Chord Player

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

Overview

Heavenly Chord Player is a local music and MIDI app from BW Innovation TW. The app does not collect, store, sell, share, or transmit personal information or user-created music data to BW Innovation TW.

Data Collection

Heavenly Chord Player does not collect user data. We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking tools, user accounts, or remote logging in the app.

Local Device Data

Heavenly Chord Player may save app settings, chord selections, output preferences, MIDI preferences, and related performance configuration on the user's device. This information is used only to make the app work and remains stored locally on the user's device.

MIDI, Audio, and Bluetooth

The app may use MIDI, audio, and Bluetooth MIDI features when the user chooses to connect local devices or other music apps. These features are used only for local music performance and routing. BW Innovation TW does not receive or store MIDI messages, audio, Bluetooth identifiers, or connected device information.

Purchases

If Heavenly Chord Player offers in-app purchases, purchase processing is handled by Apple through the App Store. BW Innovation TW does not receive or store the user's payment card information.

Children's Privacy

Heavenly Chord Player does not knowingly collect information from children or any other users.

Changes

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new last updated date.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact bw.innovation.tw@gmail.com or visit the support page.
