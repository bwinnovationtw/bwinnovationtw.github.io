RazielReveal

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Summary

RazielReveal is designed to process selected audio on the user's device. BW Innovation TW does not collect, transmit, sell, share, or use audio or personal information for advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Audio Files

When the user chooses an audio file through the iOS Files picker, RazielReveal makes a private local copy so it can separate and play the audio. The resulting stems are also stored in RazielReveal's private local app storage. Audio separation and playback occur on the device; RazielReveal does not upload audio to BW Innovation TW servers.

Data Collection and Tracking

RazielReveal does not collect personal information, usage analytics, device identifiers, diagnostics, location, contacts, or payment information. It contains no advertising, tracking, account system, analytics SDK, or remote logging service.

Permissions

Version 1.0 uses the system Files picker only when the user chooses to import audio. It does not access the microphone, camera, contacts, location, photo library, or health data.

Retention and Deletion

Imported audio and generated stems stay in RazielReveal's private local storage so recent work can be restored. Delete the RazielReveal app from the device to remove this locally stored app data. RazielReveal has no server-side copy to delete.

Children

RazielReveal does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If RazielReveal's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before the changed version is released.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, contact bw.innovation.tw@gmail.com or visit the support page.
