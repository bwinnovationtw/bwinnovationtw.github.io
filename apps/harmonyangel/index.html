For MIDI performers

Chord slots, progressions, harmony shaping, and AUv3.

HarmonyAngel follows the active chord while you perform. Choose chord slots on two pages, play from a MIDI controller or the on-screen piano, and generate descending chord tones below each incoming note.

The app is designed for keyboardists, composers, producers, worship musicians, live performers, and anyone who wants fast chord-aware harmony from simple MIDI input.

  • Two pages of 12 editable chord slots, including no-harmony slots.
  • Built-in chord types, slash roots, and custom chord definitions.
  • Progression controls for stepping through saved chord sequences.
  • Harmony count, root alignment, skip-first-harmony, and revoice controls.
  • Standalone CoreMIDI use, built-in sine synth testing, and AUv3 MIDI processor support.

Local-first privacy

HarmonyAngel does not collect user information.

No analytics No tracking, account, or usage analytics are collected.
Local MIDI and audio MIDI and audio are used only for local performance features.
Device storage Settings and musical data stay on the user's device.
