About the studio

Creative music apps from Taipei, Taiwan.

BW Innovation TW is a studio shaped by musicians, designers, and software developers. Our work sits where musical intuition meets practical engineering.

The earlier BW Innovation archive introduced PianoAngel, an iOS app made to help people play piano beautifully and easily. The current app lineup continues that same direction: making chord performance, MIDI harmony, and expressive touch control more approachable.

Apps

New tools for chord-aware performance.

HarmonyAngel

MIDI harmony processor

Turn one MIDI note into chord-aware harmony. Select chord slots, step through progressions, shape voicings, and use HarmonyAngel standalone or as an AUv3 MIDI processor.

Heavenly Chord Player

Expressive chord performer

Choose rich chords and slash roots, then perform chord tones from the iPhone or iPad screen. Optional Lightpad Block M support adds expressive hardware control.
